Who We Are

Great Time Victims is a community organisation dedicated to empowering girls and young women through education, mentorship, counselling, leadership development, life skills, and community support. We believe every girl deserves the opportunity to live with confidence, dignity, and hope.

📌 Background & Proven Impact

✔️ Verified Track Record:

Great Time Victims was officially launched on 26 December 2023 through an inaugural community outreach in the Ripon Slum, Jinja. The initiative combined open-air counseling, mentorship, and the distribution of reusable sanitary kits to vulnerable young women and girls, marking the beginning of the organization's commitment to healing, empowerment, and social transformation.
