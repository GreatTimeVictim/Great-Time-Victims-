About Great Time Victims

Great Time Victims is a community-based organisation dedicated to restoring hope, empowering vulnerable girls, and creating opportunities through education, mentorship, counselling, leadership and advocacy.

Our Mission

To empower vulnerable girls through education, counselling, mentorship and leadership development.

Our Vision

A world where every girl grows in safety, confidence, dignity and equal opportunity.

Our Programs

We provide holistic support that empowers girls to overcome challenges, discover their potential and become future leaders.

Education Support

Providing school fees assistance, scholastic materials and educational guidance to vulnerable girls.

Mentorship

Connecting girls with experienced mentors who inspire confidence, leadership and personal growth.

Counselling

Offering psychosocial support and counselling services that promote healing and emotional wellbeing.

Skills Training

Equipping girls with vocational, entrepreneurial and life skills to become financially independent.

Our Impact

Every girl reached represents hope restored and a brighter future.
